# Managing players

The **Joueurs** screen lets you create the people who play, change their details and remove them from the list. Open it from the home page with the **Joueurs** link, or go to the address `/joueurs`.

## Create a player

1. Press **Nouveau joueur**.
2. Type the **Nom** (required). Each name can be used only once, whatever the capital letters: "Alice" and "alice" are the same player. If the name is already taken, the message _« Un joueur porte déjà ce nom. Choisissez un autre nom. »_ appears and nothing is saved.
3. Optionally type a **Surnom** (nickname).
4. Optionally add a photo (see below).
5. Press **Enregistrer le joueur**.

Without a photo, the player is shown with the first letter of their name inside a coloured ring.

## Add a photo

- **Take a photo**: when the app is opened over a secure connection (https), the camera opens in the round frame. Press the big round button to take the photo. The small button on the right switches between the front and back cameras. Press the big button again to retake it.
- **Over a normal connection (http, the usual case at home)**: browsers do not allow the live camera. The big round button then opens your phone or tablet's own camera instead, and the photo you take is used. Everything else works the same.
- **Pick from the gallery**: press the small button on the left and choose a picture.
- **Remove the photo**: press **Supprimer la photo** under the buttons.

Photos are reduced automatically (no larger than 512 pixels), so you can use any picture.

## Edit a player

In the list, press **Modifier** next to the player. Change the name, nickname or photo, then press **Enregistrer le joueur**.

## Archive a player

Press **Archiver** next to the player and confirm. The player disappears from the list used to choose who plays. Their past games are kept, and so are their statistics (see [Statistics and game history](statistics.md)).
