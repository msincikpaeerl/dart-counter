# Statistics and game history

The **Statistiques** screen shows how each player is doing and lets you look back at past games. Open it from the home page with the **Statistiques et historique** link, or go to the address `/statistiques`. It works the same on a phone and on a tablet.

Every type of game is covered: **X01**, **Tour du monde** and **Bataille**.

Only **finished** games count: a game that was left unfinished, or abandoned, never appears in the statistics nor in the history.

## Filtering by type of game

Under the **Joueurs** and **Historique** tabs, a row of buttons lets you choose what to look at: **Tous**, **X01**, **Tour du monde** or **Bataille**. The list of players, a player's page and the history all follow your choice.

## A player's statistics

1. Open **Statistiques**. The **Joueurs** tab lists every player who has finished at least one game (of the chosen type), with the number of games.
2. Press a player to see their page.

The figures are shown in **one block per type and per combination of options**. They are never added together, so you can compare like with like. For example:

- **X01 · 501 · Double out** and **X01 · 501 · Simple out** are two separate blocks, and so are 301, 501 and 701.
- **Tour du monde · Débutant** and **Tour du monde · Professionnel** are two separate blocks.
- **Bataille · 500 points de vie**, **1000** and **2000** are separate blocks.

For X01, the finish counts as the one the player had to make: a player with **Double out forcé** on their profile is counted under double out.

Every block shows:

- **Parties jouées**: finished games the player took part in.
- **Parties gagnées**: games the player won. In Bataille, everyone in the winning team wins.
- **% de victoires**: games won divided by games played.

X01 blocks also show:

- **Moyenne 3 fléchettes**: the points scored divided by the darts thrown, times 3. Every dart thrown counts, including those of a visit that went **bust**: a bust visit scores 0 points but its darts are still counted, so busting lowers the average.
- **Meilleure volée**: the highest score in one visit (three darts).
- **180**: how many visits scored the maximum, 180.
- **Volées à 100+**: how many visits scored 100 points or more (the 180s are included).
- **Plus haut checkout**: the highest finishing visit of a game the player won. A dash means the player has not won yet.

Under the figures, the page lists the finished games of this player.

## The game history

The **Historique** tab lists all finished games, the most recent first. Each line shows the **date and time**, the **type and options** of the game (for example “X01 · 501 · Simple out”, “Tour du monde · Débutant” or “Bataille · 500 points de vie”), the **players** and the **winner** (the winning team for Bataille).

Press a game to open it. For an X01 game it shows every visit in order: the round, who threw, the three darts (for example _T20 · T19 · D2_), the points scored and the score left. A bust visit is shown in red with the word « Bust » and scores 0; the winning visit is shown in green. For the other types, the page shows the summary of the game. The back arrow returns to the list you came from.

## Archived players

Archiving a player (see [Managing players](players.md)) only removes them from the list used to choose who plays. Their statistics and their games stay in **Statistiques** and **Historique**, marked « archivé ».
